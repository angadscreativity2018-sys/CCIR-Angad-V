2. THE DNS LIFE CYCLE WE ARE MODELLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 From the standard formation diagram:

   ZAMS binary
     -> RLO (mass transfer 1)         primary loses envelope
     -> He-star + SN -> NS1           FIRST supernova  (kick 1)
     -> HMXB / Be X-ray binary        eccentric NS + massive companion
     -> Common Envelope (CE)          orbit shrinks HARD, circularises
     -> NS1 + He-star
     -> Case BB RLO                   companion is ULTRA-STRIPPED onto NS1
     -> Ultra-stripped SN -> NS2      SECOND supernova  (small kick, small mass loss)
     -> recycled + young NS  =  DNS

 The two physical levers that set the final (P, e) of a DNS are therefore:
   (a) the CE + Case BB phase  -> shrinks and circularises the orbit
   (b) the ultra-stripped 2nd SN -> only a little mass is lost, so the
       Blaauw (symmetric mass-loss) eccentricity is small, plus a modest
       natal kick that produces the high-e tail.

 In the code these map onto the "MT2" (mass-transfer-2) block and the
 "second supernova kick" block.


3. PROBLEM 1 - THE ARTIFICIAL LEFT-HAND BARRIER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Symptom: the (P, e) scatter showed a sharp vertical wall at P ~ 1.5 days,
 even with fully isotropic kicks (KICK_ANGLE = 180, KICK2_ANGLE = 180).
 The observed short-period DNS sat to the LEFT of this wall, unreachable.

 Diagnosis (by instrumenting the period at each stage over 20,000 runs):
   - The MT2 step shrinks the period:  period *= MT2_PERIOD_FACTOR (=0.1).
   - It was then clamped by a HARDCODED floor:  max(period, 1.0/365.25),
     i.e. exactly 1 day.
   - 25.4% of all systems piled up exactly on that 1-day floor.
   - The kickless second SN (Blaauw mass loss only) then widened that pile-up
     to ~1.48 days -> the visible wall.

 Fix: replaced the hardcoded 1-day floor with a real, low config knob.

     NEW:  MT2_PERIOD_FLOOR_DAYS = 0.03          (main.py)
     line: period = max(period * MT2_PERIOD_FACTOR,
                        MT2_PERIOD_FLOOR_DAYS / 365.25)

 Result (verified):
     minimum period   1.48 d  ->  ~0.03-0.25 d
     systems clamped  25.4%   ->  0%
 The wall disappeared and the distribution now reaches into the observed cloud.


4. PROBLEM 2 - CLOUD IN THE WRONG PLACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Before tuning, the simulated cloud sat at median P ~ 8.6 d and median e ~ 0.47
 - i.e. orbits too WIDE and too ECCENTRIC compared with real DNS:

     metric            observed     sim (before)
     median P           0.80 d        8.6 d      (10x too wide)
     median e           0.18          0.47       (2.6x too high)
     frac P < 1 day      0.50         0.09
     frac e < 0.4        0.83         0.39

 Root causes, read straight off the life-cycle diagram:
   - The companion was NOT ultra-stripped enough, so the 2nd SN lost a lot of
     mass -> large Blaauw eccentricity (~0.44, the dense streak in old plots).
   - The CE / Case BB orbit shrink was too weak (period factor too large).
   - Too much of the first-SN eccentricity survived into the DNS.


5. PARAMETER SCAN AND FINAL FIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 A scan over (MT2_DELTA_M, MT2_ECC_FACTOR, MT2_PERIOD_FACTOR) and then the
 second-SN kick (KICK2_V_NS, KICK2_CORE_MASS_FRAC, KICK2_SCATTER_SIGMA) was run,
 comparing simulated and observed quantiles each time.

 Key insight from the scan: KICK2_V_NS initially did NOTHING. The Mandel kick is
     v = KICK2_V_NS * (M_CO2 - M_rem) / M_rem * (1 + scatter),  clamped at 0.
 Because MT2 ultra-strips the companion to ~1.6 Msun, its CO core
 (0.6 * 1.6 = 0.96 Msun) was LIGHTER than the 1.3 Msun remnant, so the kick
 clamped to zero no matter how large KICK2_V_NS was. Raising
 KICK2_CORE_MASS_FRAC to 0.95 makes the core exceed the remnant and reactivates
 the kick, which is what produces the high-e tail.

 FINAL PARAMETERS (main.py):

     # ultra-stripped 2nd SN (Case BB RLO):
     MT2_DELTA_M           = 8.4     # strips companion 10 -> 1.6 Msun
     MT2_EFFICIENCY        = 0.001   # almost nothing accreted by NS1
     MT2_ECC_FACTOR        = 0.4     # CE circularises (was 0.8)
     MT2_PERIOD_FACTOR     = 0.02    # CE shrinks orbit hard (was 0.1)
     MT2_PERIOD_FLOOR_DAYS = 0.03    # floor below observed minimum 0.078 d

     # natal kick of the ultra-stripped SN (makes the high-e systems):
     KICK2_V_NS            = 150.0   # km/s   (was 0)
     KICK2_CORE_MASS_FRAC  = 0.95    # so CO core > remnant -> kick is active
     KICK2_SCATTER_SIGMA   = 0.3
     KICK2_ANGLE           = 180     # isotropic

     # first SN kept isotropic:
     KICK_ANGLE            = 180


6. FINAL RESULT  (30,000 runs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     metric              observed     simulated
     median period        0.80 d       0.81 d
     10-90% period range  0.14-14 d    0.11-14 d
     median eccentricity  0.175        0.201
     mean eccentricity    0.252        0.232
     frac P < 1 day        0.50        0.55
     frac e < 0.4          0.83        0.88

 The period distribution matches almost exactly. Eccentricities now cluster
 low (0.1-0.4) with a tail toward 0.6, instead of the old blob at e ~ 0.4.

 Remaining minor gap: the observed high-e tail (3 of 18 systems near e ~ 0.6)
 is slightly fatter than the simulation's. This is within small-sample noise;
 nudging KICK2_V_NS up toward ~200 thickens the tail if a closer match is wanted.


7. GROUNDING THE KICKS AND MASSES IN PUBLISHED SIMULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 The hand-tuned kicks/masses were then replaced with values taken from the
 literature so the model reflects the actual DNS formation channel:

   FIRST SN  (forms the recycled NS; left column of the life-cycle diagram)
     - Normally-stripped He-star -> CCSN / ECSN.
     - Kick: bimodal Maxwellian (Verbunt, Igoshev & Cator 2017; Vigna-Gomez 2018):
         KICK1_SIGMA_LOW  = 30 km/s   (ECSN / low-mass, ~42%)
         KICK1_SIGMA_HIGH = 265 km/s  (CCSN)
       Consistent with Müller, Tauris et al. (2019) Table 2 CCSN models
       (z9.6, z12: 21-64 km/s low;  s11.8: 278 km/s).
     - Remnant mass POST_IMPULSE_MASS1 = 1.35 Msun (recycled DNS pulsar).
     - IMPULSE_DIST = "bimodal".

   SECOND SN  (ultra-stripped; right column: Case BB RLO -> ultra-stripped SN)
     - Companion ultra-stripped to a ~1.5-1.6 Msun CO core by MT2 (Case BB RLO).
     - Kick: bimodal Maxwellian grounded in Müller, Tauris et al. (2019) Table 2
       ULTRA-STRIPPED models:
         he2.8 -> 11 km/s          (low component)
         he3.5 -> 238 km/s,  he3.0 -> 695 km/s   (high component)
       Implemented as KICK2_DIST = "bimodal":
         KICK2_SIGMA_LOW  = 30 km/s   (he2.8-like)
         KICK2_SIGMA_HIGH = 200 km/s  (he3.0/he3.5-like)
         KICK2_LOW_FRAC   = 0.8
     - Remnant mass POST_IMPULSE_MASS2 = 1.33 Msun (he-model mean Mgrav).

   KEY PHYSICS:  ultra-stripped SN kicks are NOT all small - the 3D sims show a
   wide spread (11 - 695 km/s).  Bound DNS form preferentially from the LOW-kick
   subset (Tauris et al. 2017), which is why the observed sample is mostly low-e.
   The occasional moderate kick from the high component produces the high-e
   systems (B1913+16, J1757-1854).  The model reproduces this self-consistently:
   high-kick systems mostly unbind, the survivors are low-e with a thin high-e tail.

   After grounding the kicks, MT2_PERIOD_FACTOR was retuned 0.035 -> 0.025 to
   restore the period match (the stronger, realistic kicks widen the orbits).

 Final match (full pipeline incl. aging):
     metric              observed     simulated
     median period        0.80 d       0.85 d
     median eccentricity  0.175        0.190
     mean eccentricity    0.252        0.242
     frac P < 1 day        0.50        0.53
     frac e < 0.4          0.83        0.83
     frac e > 0.5 (tail)   0.17        0.11

 Sources:
   Müller, Tauris, Heger et al. 2019, MNRAS 484, 3307  (arXiv:1811.05483)
   Tauris et al. 2017, ApJ 846, 170  (arXiv:1706.09438)
   Vigna-Gómez et al. 2018, MNRAS 481, 4009  (arXiv:1805.07974)
   Verbunt, Igoshev & Cator 2017, A&A 608, A57


8. HOW TO REPRODUCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - In plot.py set OBS_DATA_SET = "dns" so the 18 green DNS diamonds are shown.
 - Run:  python main.py
 - SHOW_ECC_STATS = True produces the (period, eccentricity) scatter plus the
   side CDF panel comparing simulated vs observed eccentricity.
